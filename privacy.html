Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Quintessence Development Solutions (QDS), Johannesburg, South Africa. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

This website does not use contact forms, tracking cookies or advertising tools. We only receive personal information when you choose to contact us, for example by email. That may include your name, contact details, organisation and anything you include in your message.

Our hosting provider and font provider may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the site.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to respond to your enquiry and, where you become a client, to deliver our services. We do not sell or share your information for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality is one of our core values. Information shared with us in the course of an enquiry or engagement is kept private and is disclosed only where required by law or with your consent.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. To do so, email info@qdsbusiness.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
